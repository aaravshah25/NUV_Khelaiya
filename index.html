01 / TRADITION

Sacred Heritage Reimagined

Every rhythm of the Dhol echoes centuries of Gujarati folklore, woven seamlessly with modern sonic depth and atmospheric lighting on our grand open campus arena.

02 / UNITY

10,000+ Unified Souls

Students, faculty, alumni, and state dignitaries gather under illuminated open skies to experience pure synchrony in the largest collegiate Garba celebration of the region.

03 / EXCELLENCE

A Festival of Distinction

Curated with meticulous precision by the NUV Cultural Committee—setting new benchmarks in festival production, security, fast-track digital verification, and hospitality.

01 — SACRED CIRCLE

GARBA

Centric concentric swirls radiating pure rhythmic euphoria. Thousands move in synchrony around the sacred shrine as bells chime and heartbeats accelerate.

02 — HIGH OCTANE

DANCE

Explosive Dandiya duels, swift footwork, and acrobatic precision. Witness traditional Raas transformed into an electrifying performing art showcase.

03 — ACOUSTIC & BASS

MUSIC

Live authentic Gujarati folk vocalists accompanied by thunderous Dhol ensembles, fusion violinists, and cutting-edge electronic sub-bass sound design.

04 — GLORY & PRESTIGE

COMPETITIONS

Judged by renowned classical artists and state choreographers. Compete for Best Traditional Attire, Master of Steps, and the prestigious NUV Khelaiya Trophy.

05 — ROYAL ATTIRE

FASHION

Handcrafted mirror-work Chaniya Cholis, royal Kedias, oxidized silver jewels, and vibrant bandhani turbans creating a visual tapestry of haute heritage.

06 — 3 NIGHTS

CELEBRATION

Midnight countdowns, luminous lasers over the campus dome, gourmet festival delicacies, and memories etched forever into university lore.

CHRONICLES OF EUPHORIA

EVENT TIMELINE

Three transformative evenings curated with distinct thematic journeys, artist lineups, and competitive rounds.

DAY 01 • 12 SEPTEMBER

THE SACRED INCEPTION

Inaugural lamp lighting, the Maha Aarti ceremony, traditional Raas kickoff, and the university-wide heritage costume showcase.

  • 06:30 PM — Grand Shubh Inauguration & Lamp Lighting
  • 07:30 PM — Maha Aarti with 1,000+ Diyas
  • 08:30 PM — Traditional 2-Taali & 3-Taali Circle Rounds
  • 11:30 PM — Day 1 Spot Costume Accolades
DAY 02 • 13 SEPTEMBER

THE FUSION STORM

High-octane fusion night combining traditional Garba rhythms with live folk-rock bands, laser lighting choreography, and celebrity performances.

  • 07:00 PM — Folk-Rock Opening Symphony
  • 08:15 PM — Inter-College Synchrony Garba Clash
  • 09:45 PM — Celebrity Guest Artist Live Set
  • 11:45 PM — Midnight Dhol Resonance Finale
DAY 03 • 14 SEPTEMBER

THE GRAND FINALE

The championship climax. Final judgment rounds, the awarding of the Mega Trophy, and an uninterrupted midnight celebration under starlight.

  • 07:00 PM — Finalists Grand Entrance Procession
  • 08:30 PM — Mega Championship Garba Decider
  • 10:45 PM — Trophy Ceremony & Cash Honors
  • 11:30 PM — Grand Midnight Finale Countdown
