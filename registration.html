FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW

The Digital Pass is an encrypted, contactless event credential granting you entry to all 3 nights of NUV Khelaiya 2026. Each pass contains a tamper-proof dynamic QR token tied to your student ID and registered profile.
Upon submitting your registration, your personalized digital pass is generated instantly on screen and dispatched to your registered email address. You can save it to Apple Wallet, Google Wallet, or retain the screenshot on your mobile device.
Gate turnstiles are equipped with high-speed optical laser scanners. Simply hold your phone screen or pass printout under the scanner beam. The system verifies your ticket in less than 200 milliseconds and unlocks entry.
Our Cultural Committee Helpdesks at Gate A, Gate B, and Gate C can manually look up your Pass ID or Student Roll Number on the spot to issue an instantaneous fast-track gate clearance.
