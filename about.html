THE CULTURAL COMMITTEE

STEWARDS OF ARTISTIC EXCELLENCE

The Cultural Committee of Navrachana University is a student-led governing council passionate about preserving Indian traditional performing arts while integrating cutting-edge production values.

From choreographing intricate Raas sequences to orchestrating state-of-the-art acoustics and digital credential systems, our team ensures every participant experiences unmatched hospitality, safety, and cultural reverence.

FOUNDATIONAL ETHOS

THE FOUR PILLARS

The guiding principles that define every edition of NUV Khelaiya.

PILLAR 01

Authentic Folklore

Upholding authentic Raas rhythms, sacred Aarti invocations, and live acoustic heritage vocalists without dilution.

PILLAR 02

Inclusive Inclusivity

Welcoming students, staff, alumni, and cross-university delegations in an environment of absolute unity and respect.

PILLAR 03

High-Octane Safety

Zero tolerance on security breaches with biometric/QR verification, dedicated female safety squads, and paramedic support.

PILLAR 04

Acoustic Perfection

Custom-engineered line array acoustic setups ensuring pristine acoustic clarity without hazardous sound pollution.
